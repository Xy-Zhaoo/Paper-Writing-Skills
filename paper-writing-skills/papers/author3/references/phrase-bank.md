# Libo Phrase Bank

Fill slots with evidence. Use these patterns for scene-grounded restoration and vision arguments.

## Problem
- `In [SCENE], [DEGRADATION] limits [TASK/OUTCOME].`
- `[TASK] is needed for [APPLICATION], where [CONSEQUENCE] matters.`
- `[SETTING] produces [ARTIFACT], reducing [QUALITY/DOWNSTREAM USE].`
- `The practical challenge is to recover [TARGET] from [DEGRADED INPUT].`

## Gap
- `Existing [METHODS] address [ADJACENT PROBLEM], but not [TARGET PHENOMENON].`
- `Prior datasets cover [KNOWN CASE], leaving [TARGET SETTING] underrepresented.`
- `[APPROACH] works under [SOURCE CONDITION], whereas [TARGET CONDITION] remains unresolved.`
- `The missing resource is [DATA/MODELING CAPABILITY] needed for [GOAL].`

## Failure
- `Directly applying [METHOD] to [SETTING] produces [FAILURE].`
- `When [ARTIFACT] overlaps with [CONTENT], [METHOD] confuses [A] with [B].`
- `Under [CONDITION], [BASELINE] loses [PROPERTY] and yields [CONSEQUENCE].`
- `Standard [REMEDY] provides limited relief because [BOTTLENECK].`

## Mechanism
- `[DEGRADATION] arises from the interaction of [FACTOR A] and [FACTOR B].`
- `The acquisition process projects [HIDDEN EFFECT] into [OBSERVABLE DOMAIN], producing [ARTIFACT].`
- `Because [CONDITION], [SIGNAL] varies with [FACTOR], hindering [INFERENCE/RESTORATION].`
- `This mechanism explains why [SOURCE METHOD] does not transfer to [TARGET SETTING].`

## Observation
- `We observe that [SIGNAL/ARTIFACT] is concentrated in [DOMAIN/REGION].`
- `[ANALYSIS] shows that [PROPERTY] changes with [CONDITION], while [INVARIANT] remains stable.`
- `The observation suggests using [COMPONENT] to exploit [PROPERTY].`
- `Agreement between [ANALYSIS] and [EXPERIMENT] supports [DESIGN CHOICE].`

## Method Transition
- `To address [CHALLENGES], we propose [METHOD] for [TASK].`
- `Motivated by [OBSERVATION], we formulate [TASK] as [FORMULATION] and introduce [METHOD].`
- `Rather than applying [PRIOR STRATEGY] directly, [METHOD] models [TARGET CHARACTERISTIC].`
- `The framework combines [IDEA A] and [IDEA B] to handle [DIFFICULTY].`

## Component Mapping
- `[COMPONENT 1] estimates [SIGNAL] to address [CHALLENGE 1].`
- `[COMPONENT 2] processes [TARGET PART] while preserving [COMPLEMENTARY PROPERTY].`
- `[COMPONENT 3] aligns [OBJECT A] with [OBJECT B], improving [TARGET].`
- `Together, the components map [CHALLENGES] to [SUPPORTED EFFECTS].`

## Practicalization
- `We construct [RESOURCE] to cover [DIVERSITY AXES] for [TRAINING/EVALUATION].`
- `The simulation models [DEGRADATION] in [DOMAIN] while preserving [PROPERTY].`
- `The acquisition or calibration pipeline reduces [BURDEN] under [CONSTRAINT].`
- `These choices support [PRACTICAL SCOPE] with [BOUNDARY].`

## Evidence
- `Experiments on [DATASET/SCENE] show [METHOD] achieves [RESULT].`
- `Compared with [BASELINE] under [SETTING], [METHOD] improves [METRIC/QUALITY].`
- `Ablation shows that [COMPONENT] contributes [SUPPORTED EFFECT].`
- `Quantitative and visual evidence support [CONCLUSION] on [SCOPE].`

## Cost/Guarantee
- `[METHOD] preserves [QUALITY] while reducing [COST], if measured.`
- `The pipeline uses [RESOURCE LEVEL] and adds [OVERHEAD].`
- `Under [HARD CONDITION], [QUALITY] remains within [BOUND] of [REFERENCE].`
- `The guarantee applies only to [EVALUATED SCOPE].`

## Scope
- `We study [ARTIFACT/TASK] under [CAPTURE/DEGRADATION SETTING].`
- `The evaluation covers [DATA/MODEL/RESOLUTION RANGE].`
- `The method targets [TARGET CASE] and does not assume [UNSUPPORTED CONDITION].`
- `Conclusions beyond [EVALUATED SCOPE] require further evidence.`

## Contribution
- `We identify [PHENOMENON] and explain its effect through [MECHANISM].`
- `We construct [RESOURCE] for [TASK/SETTING].`
- `We propose [METHOD] with [COMPONENTS] mapped to [CHALLENGES].`
- `We demonstrate [RESULT] on [SCOPE] with [PRACTICAL PROPERTY].`
