# JZ Phrase Bank

Fill slots with supplied evidence. These are syntax patterns, not source-paper sentences.

## Problem
- `[COMPONENT] becomes the dominant cost in [SETTING] because [SCALING FACTOR].`
- `At [SCALE], [OPERATION] limits [PRACTICAL OBJECTIVE] through [COST].`
- `The target regime requires [CAPABILITY] under [CONSTRAINT].`
- `[TASK] is bottlenecked by [COMPONENT] when [CONDITION].`

## Gap
- `[PRIOR_METHOD] handles [COVERED CASE], but leaves [TARGET OBJECT] in [UNRESOLVED STATE].`
- `Existing [METHOD FAMILY] optimizes [A], whereas [B] remains [UNSUPPORTED/EXPENSIVE].`
- `Directly extending [PRIOR_METHOD] to [SETTING] leaves [GAP].`
- `The relevant gap is [SPECIFIC GAP], rather than [BROADER CLAIM].`

## Failure
- `Applying [PRIOR_METHOD] to [SETTING] causes [FAILURE].`
- `At [REGIME], [BASELINE] changes [METRIC/QUALITY] from [A] to [B].`
- `The direct solution reaches only [OBSERVED LIMIT] under [CONDITION].`
- `The failure persists across [EVALUATED SCOPE], indicating [LIMITATION].`

## Mechanism
- `[FAILURE] arises because [MECHANISM] couples [A] with [B].`
- `[MISMATCH] distorts [INTERMEDIATE QUANTITY], which propagates to [FINAL ERROR].`
- `The issue is not [SYMPTOM]; [CAUSE] makes [TARGET] difficult.`
- `Because [CONDITION], [FACTOR] controls [SHARED QUANTITY] and produces [EFFECT].`

## Observation
- `We observe that [STRUCTURE] remains [PROPERTY] under [SETTING].`
- `[ANALYSIS] reveals [OBSERVATION], explaining [PRIOR FAILURE].`
- `This pattern suggests applying [MECHANISM A] to [PART A] and [MECHANISM B] to [PART B].`
- `The observation turns [BROAD PROBLEM] into [PRECISE DESIGN QUESTION].`

## Method Transition
- `Based on this analysis, we propose [METHOD] to [CORE ACTION].`
- `To address [GAP], [METHOD] [ORGANIZING PRINCIPLE].`
- `This finding motivates [METHOD], which preserves [CONSTRAINT] while [ACTION].`
- `Rather than [NAIVE OPTION], we [METHOD DECISION] because [MECHANISTIC REASON].`

## Component Mapping
- `To address [CHALLENGE 1], [COMPONENT 1] [ACTION], yielding [LOCAL EFFECT].`
- `[COMPONENT 2] targets [CHALLENGE 2] by [ACTION].`
- `[COMPONENT] isolates [ERROR/SIGNAL] in [LOCATION] instead of [NAIVE BEHAVIOR].`
- `Together, [COMPONENTS] connect [MECHANISM] to [SYSTEM-LEVEL EFFECT].`

## Practicalization
- `[METHOD] is implemented on [HARDWARE/PIPELINE] without [COST], when verified.`
- `The design absorbs [TRANSFORM] into [MODULE], avoiding runtime [OPERATION].`
- `The added [COMPONENT] costs [COST] while preserving [QUALITY].`
- `The method is plug-and-play for [SCOPE] under [BOUNDARY].`

## Evidence
- `On [MODEL/TASK] with [SETTING], [METHOD] achieves [RESULT] over [BASELINE].`
- `[METHOD] reduces [COST] from [A] to [B] while retaining [QUALITY].`
- `Ablations show that [COMPONENT] contributes [LOCAL RESULT].`
- `The end-to-end result is [RESULT] on [HARDWARE/SCOPE].`

## Cost/Guarantee
- `[METHOD] maintains [QUALITY] with [COST].`
- `The gain requires [TRAINING/MODIFICATION] and adds [OVERHEAD].`
- `At [SETTING], [QUALITY] remains within [BOUND] of [BASELINE].`
- `The claim is limited to [SCOPE] because [BOUNDARY].`

## Scope
- `We evaluate [METHOD] on [MODELS/TASKS] under [HARDWARE/REGIME].`
- `The evidence covers [DATA/SEQUENCE/RESOLUTION RANGE].`
- `The method targets [INFERENCE/TRAINING] rather than [EXCLUDED SETTING].`
- `Claims beyond [EVALUATED SCOPE] require additional evidence.`

## Contribution
- `We identify [MECHANISM] as the cause of [FAILURE] in [SETTING].`
- `We propose [METHOD], which maps [COMPONENTS] to [CHALLENGES].`
- `We provide [ANALYSIS/IMPLEMENTATION] with [SUPPORTED PROPERTY].`
- `We demonstrate [RESULT] on [SCOPE] with [COST/GUARANTEE].`
