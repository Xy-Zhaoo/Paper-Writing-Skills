# KL Phrase Bank

Fill slots with evidence. Keep the hidden factor and deployment contract visible.

## Problem
- `[MODEL/TECHNIQUE] provides [CAPABILITY], but [COST] blocks [DEPLOYMENT SETTING].`
- `At [HARD REGIME], [REPEATED OPERATION] makes [TASK] impractical.`
- `Practical use requires [CAPABILITY] under [RESOURCE CONSTRAINT].`
- `[TASK] fails to meet [DEPLOYMENT REQUIREMENT] when [CONDITION].`

## Gap
- `[PRIOR_DIRECTION] is attractive because [PRACTICAL BENEFIT], yet [TARGET REGIME] remains unresolved.`
- `Existing [METHODS] address [EASIER CASE], leaving [HARD CASE] exposed.`
- `The missing capability is [SPECIFIC CAPABILITY], not [GENERAL LIMITATION].`
- `[ASSUMPTION] holds in [SOURCE SETTING] but breaks in [TARGET SETTING].`

## Failure
- `Under [HARD REGIME], [METHOD] produces [OBSERVED FAILURE].`
- `As [CONTROL VARIABLE] increases, [ERROR] accumulates and appears as [QUALITY LOSS].`
- `The direct transfer of [PRIOR_METHOD] causes [FAILURE] without [REQUIRED PROPERTY].`
- `[BASELINE] remains competitive on [EASY SETTING] but degrades on [TARGET SETTING].`

## Mechanism
- `The overlooked factor is [FACTOR], which determines [INTERMEDIATE QUANTITY].`
- `[MISMATCH] lets [UNDESIRED SHORTCUT/ERROR] pass through [PROCESS].`
- `Existing methods entangle [A] and [B], so neither can be controlled independently.`
- `Because [FACTOR] changes under [CONDITION], [STANDARD PROCEDURE] no longer represents [ACTUAL STATE].`

## Observation
- `We find that [FACTOR] varies with [CONDITION], while [INVARIANT] remains stable.`
- `Decomposing [QUANTITY] into [A] and [B] reveals distinct error behavior.`
- `This observation explains why [PRIOR_METHOD] fails in [HARD REGIME].`
- `The result suggests controlling [TERM A] and [TERM B] separately.`

## Method Transition
- `Based on this observation, we propose [METHOD] to control [FACTOR/ERROR] explicitly.`
- `Building on [DECOMPOSITION], [METHOD] [ORGANIZING ACTION].`
- `To bridge [GAP], we design [METHOD] around [HIDDEN FACTOR].`
- `Rather than [NAIVE APPROACH], [METHOD] [DESIGN DECISION] because [MECHANISM].`

## Component Mapping
- `First, [COMPONENT 1] estimates [SIGNAL] to address [CHALLENGE 1].`
- `Next, [COMPONENT 2] transforms [OBJECT] so that [EFFECT].`
- `Finally, [COMPONENT 3] compensates [ERROR] without [DEPLOYMENT COST].`
- `[COMPONENT] separates [TERM A] from [TERM B], enabling [CONTROL].`

## Practicalization
- `The transformation is absorbed into [MODULE], avoiding runtime [OPERATION].`
- `[METHOD] requires no [RETRAINING/ARCHITECTURE CHANGE/EXTRA EVALUATION], when verified.`
- `We retain [CAPABILITY] with [OVERHEAD] on [RESOURCE].`
- `The design supports [DEPLOYMENT SETTING] under [BOUNDARY].`

## Evidence
- `On [MODEL/TASK] at [REGIME], [METHOD] achieves [RESULT] over [BASELINE].`
- `Across [SCOPE], [METHOD] improves [METRIC] while preserving [CAPABILITY].`
- `The hard-regime result is [RESULT] with [SPEED/MEMORY/QUALITY].`
- `Ablation confirms that [COMPONENT] controls [MECHANISM/ERROR].`

## Cost/Guarantee
- `[METHOD] preserves [QUALITY] without [COST], if documented.`
- `The method adds [OVERHEAD] and requires [TRAINING/RESOURCE].`
- `At [REGIME], [METRIC] remains within [BOUND] of [REFERENCE].`
- `The guarantee applies to [SCOPE] under [CONDITION].`

## Scope
- `We evaluate [METHOD] on [MODELS/TASKS] under [HARD REGIME].`
- `The claim covers [HARDWARE/DATA/SEQUENCE RANGE].`
- `The method targets [DEPLOYMENT MODE] and does not establish [UNSUPPORTED CLAIM].`
- `Generalization beyond [EVALUATED SCOPE] remains untested.`

## Contribution
- `We identify [FACTOR] as the source of [FAILURE] in [REGIME].`
- `We derive [METHOD] from this mechanism and implement [COMPONENTS].`
